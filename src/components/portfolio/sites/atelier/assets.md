# ATELIER & MAISON image provenance

These two authored portfolio demonstrations use custom AI-generated editorial-style concept imagery. The generated images are not photographs of completed client commissions or an operating retreat.

- `public/images/showcase/atelier-courtyard.jpg`, `atelier-interior.jpg`, `atelier-pavilion.jpg`: generated architectural concept imagery for ATELIER.
- `public/images/showcase/maison-hero.jpg`, `maison-suite.jpg`, `maison-bath.jpg`, `maison-table.jpg`: generated hospitality concept imagery for MAISON.
- `public/images/showcase/maison-hero.webp`: WebP export from `maison-hero.jpg` via Sharp for the parent showcase and MAISON hero.
- `public/images/showcase/atelier-detail.webp`: pre-existing licensed image-search asset provided with the project, used in ATELIER galleries. Original provenance is maintained by the parent project.

Room rates are illustrative. No availability lookup or reservation is performed.
